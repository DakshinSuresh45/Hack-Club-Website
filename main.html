WELCOME TO HACK CLUB

Code, Build & Ship Projects Together.

Hack Club is a network of high school maker communities. We are an entirely student-led coding environment where you don't just learn syntax—you build real products and launch them to the world.

Key Information Highlights

MEETING TIMES

Every Thursday @ 3:30 PM

We gather weekly in Computer Lab 204 for live coding sessions, hardware hacking, and project feedback.

HARDWARE & GRANTS

Free Hardware & Subdomains

Members get access to free Raspberry Pis, Arduinos, microcontrollers, and custom domain grants to host their apps.

NO EXPERIENCE REQUIRED

100% Beginner Friendly

Whether you've never written a line of code or build full-stack apps, everyone is welcome to join and learn.

What We Do at Hack Club

At Hack Club, we believe the best way to learn to code is by building things you care about. Instead of standard classroom lectures, every session is structured like a mini hackathon where you get to work on your own ideas.

  • Build Web Apps & Mobile Games
  • Ship Open Source Software
  • Participate in Global Hackathons
> HOW_TO_JOIN.sh

3. Pick a project idea and start shipping code!

Who We Are

CincyHacks is an annual hackathon organized entirely by high school students from Hack Club. We aim to empower students across the Greater Cincinnati area by providing a collaborative space, mentorship, and resources to build software and hardware projects.

What We Want to Achieve

  • More CincyHacks Attendees: Expand reach across 30+ regional high schools.
  • More Growth: Increase workshop variety including AI, Game Dev, and Hardware.
  • More Reach: Connect students directly with industry mentors and local tech sponsors.
